Placeholder for Austrian OpenStreetMap PBF extracts.

For the Docker Compose Nominatim/dev service, download the current Geofabrik Austria
extract as:

    TOOLS/osm-data/austria-latest.osm.pbf

Source:
https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/austria-latest.osm.pbf

For the historical routing pipeline, also place the yearly Austria snapshots in this
folder with these filenames:

    TOOLS/osm-data/austria-150101.osm.pbf
    TOOLS/osm-data/austria-160101.osm.pbf
    TOOLS/osm-data/austria-170101.osm.pbf
    TOOLS/osm-data/austria-180101.osm.pbf

    TOOLS/osm-data/austria-200101.osm.pbf
